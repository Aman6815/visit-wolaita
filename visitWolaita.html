ABOUT WOLaita

A place of nature, culture and history

Wolaita is an area of southern Ethiopia known for its distinctive culture, productive highlands, beautiful landscapes and strong agricultural traditions.

Wolaita Sodo serves as the administrative center of Wolaita Zone and provides a convenient starting point for exploring the surrounding destinations.
